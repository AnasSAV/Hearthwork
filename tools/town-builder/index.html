How to build

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the town. Click one to place several.
  • Green means it fits, red means it doesn't.
  • Drag a placed piece to move it. Drag empty ground to look around.
  • Del removes, D duplicates, Esc stops placing.
  • Ground: paint grass, paving, water or sand. Shift+drag fills a box.
  • Mouse wheel zooms. Save town updates the game.
